# The H.E.L.P. suite and AskVic — decided Nov 2, 2026

Rob asked whether to move the whole H.E.L.P. (Human Equation Leadership Psychology)
suite off drrobfurman.com and into AskVic, as a selling point for the combined set of
tools. This records the answer and the reasoning, because the reasoning is the part
worth having in three weeks.

**Short version: connect them, do not merge them, and not yet. And the business logic
probably runs the other way round from how the question was framed.**

## What H.E.L.P. actually is, as it stands today

Live at `drrobfurman.com/human-equation-suite`. It is more built than a side project.

- **The 8 Factors** — a behavioural vocabulary for leadership under pressure:
  Regulation Under Pressure, Human Awareness, Trust Construction, Reality Anchoring,
  Gray Area Leadership, Team & Systems Leadership, Instructional & Academic Leadership,
  Vision & Change Leadership. Not a personality test; the page is explicit about that,
  and it is right to be.
- **Leadership Pressure Diagnostic** — sets a baseline profile and first growth edges.
  The recommended entry point.
- **Two simulations** — "A Day in the Life of a School Principal" and "A Day in the Life
  of an Urban Student". The principal sim takes written responses to instruction,
  behaviour, staff and parent situations, coaches tone and decision-making as you go, and
  ends with a summary of leadership patterns rather than a score.
- **The evidence profile / dashboard** — diagnostic, course and simulation results
  accumulating into one record over time.
- **Conversation Mastery Lab** — a 30-day programme on elicitation, rapport, questioning,
  reflective listening and difficult conversations.

**The valuable bone is the evidence profile, not the course.** A course is a course.
A longitudinal, dated record of a leader's practice under pressure is a thing almost
nobody sells, and it is the piece that earns its way into AskVic.

## Why not a migration

**1. AskVic's student data is not trustworthy yet, and that is the live risk.**
See "Open right now — Oct 27" in `what-we-are-building.md`. The verdict stream records
success and stays silent on difficulty, so an on-level card built today reads 100% and
the red-flag children are the ones missing from the table. The first principal who looks
closely will find the data does not say what it appears to say. Starting a second product
before that is solid is how two things end up 70% done, and 70% done is worth nothing to
a school.

**2. It fights the thesis settled on Oct 21.** Designer is the front door, VIC is the
heart, the home page sells Designer first. That clarity was hard won. A third pillar
called leadership psychology makes the five-minute demo ambiguous again, and "is this a
student instruction tool or a leadership thing?" is the worst question a teacher can ask
in minute two.

**3. The name collides with the product's own help.** AskVic is getting a help/how-to
button (overview, startup guide, "I'm stuck"). A principal looking for help should not
land on a leadership psychology suite. Whatever else happens, H.E.L.P. needs a different
label inside AskVic.

## The reframe: H.E.L.P. is not a selling point for VIC, it may be the revenue

The question was posed as using H.E.L.P. to sell the VIC suite. The argument for turning
that around:

- Everything in AskVic is free today, with charging planned later. **Teachers do not pay
  for software.** The plan as it stands produces adoption and no revenue.
- **Principals have a PD budget line**, leadership coaching has an established price, and
  schools already spend heavily on consultants doing a thinner version of the principal
  sim. The buyer and the user are the same person, which removes the entire approval
  chain that kills most ed-tech deals.
- An evidence profile a principal can put toward their own recertification has concrete,
  defensible value. Rob is assembling exactly such a packet right now, so the worth of it
  is not hypothetical.

So: **VIC gets you in the building; leadership practice is what the school plan charges
for.** Teacher Designer and student VIC stay free and drive adoption.

The positioning line, which no competitor can currently say:

> AskVic is the only place where the principal's growth and their teachers' growth live
> in the same system.

Every ed-tech company sells a principal a tool for managing other people. Almost nobody
sells them something that develops them.

## Agreed order of work

1. **Fix the attempt denominator first.** Record the attempt, not only the verdict, so
   "not met" becomes a fact rather than an absence. Nothing about H.E.L.P. starts before
   this. It is what makes the principal's dashboard honest.
2. **Connect, do not migrate.** One login across askvic.ai and the suite, with H.E.L.P.
   appearing as a module inside the leadership workspace that already exists. A shared
   account and a door — not a code migration. Much less work, most of the benefit.
3. **Keep drrobfurman.com as the funnel.** The free Pressure Diagnostic is a strong lead
   magnet, and the site carries the speaking and publications credibility. Gutting it to
   feed AskVic would cost the top of the funnel and the credibility page at once.
4. **Wire the one integration that is more than colocation:** AskVic's classroom
   walkthrough tool and Factor 7, Instructional & Academic Leadership. A principal doing
   real walkthroughs is practising that factor in the building, not in a scenario.
   Evidence from actual work beats evidence from a simulation, and it is not copyable by
   anyone without both halves.

## Cautions worth carrying forward

- **The 30-day Conversation Mastery Lab is a commitment product.** Completion rates for
  those are poor across the board. Fine as a product; risky as a headline selling point,
  because "30 days" reads as work rather than value.
- **Two buyers, two sales motions.** VIC sells against acute, budgeted pain — the sub did
  not show up, instruction drops to zero. Leadership PD sells against a different budget
  and a slower decision. Bundling the nice-to-have with the must-have can dilute the
  must-have pitch if the pitch is not kept separate for each audience.

## Still open

- What H.E.L.P. is called inside AskVic.
- Whether the leadership module is included in a school plan or priced separately.
- Whether the simulations run inside AskVic or stay hosted where they are behind a shared
  login (the cheaper answer, and probably the first one).
- Whether a teacher — not only a leader — gets the 8 Factors material, given that the
  urban-student simulation was built as staff PD.
