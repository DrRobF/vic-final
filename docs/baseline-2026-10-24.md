## The one number that decides the sub-coverage claim

**Day slots with a lesson attached, as a share of day slots: 30 / 85.**

Sub coverage can only put a lesson on screen if the class's own teacher planned one and gave
it a standard and objective. So that share *is* the probability that "when your sub doesn't
show, the lesson still happens" is true on a given day. At 30/85 it is true about a third of
the time; the mechanism works, the claim does not hold yet.

This is the number to watch weekly. It is also the number that turns the principal's
"9 of 11 plans sent" from an admin stat into a risk statement: the gap is not paperwork, it
is classes that would be lost if that teacher called in sick tomorrow.

## What else first real use should produce

These are all zero now, so any movement is unambiguous:

- **Lessons pushed to students** — the VIC Room's Launch button being used at all.
- **VIC verdicts** — evidence for the principal's on-level and red-flag card. Nothing can be
  said about students being on level until this is non-zero.
- **Cover slips** — whether sub coverage gets used in anger or stays a demo.

## What would make the number move

Not nagging. The argument that does not need Rob in it: **your plan is your sub plan.** A
teacher who plans is a teacher whose class is not wasted when they are out, and who does not
return to a wrecked week. That is a reason to plan that survives Rob not asking.
